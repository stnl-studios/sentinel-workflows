# stnl-slice-executor

## Purpose

Run one explicitly selected EXECUTE_SLICE or APPLY_FINDINGS operation on its exact current EC-1 handoff. Read the requirements, approved Plan and Task, referenced code/tests and the evidence needed for that operation. Preserve requirements, normative preparation, other deliveries and completed proof. Preparation review and implementation checks do not confer formal PASS.

### Domain guidance

For a technical approved slice, inspect the native skill catalog descriptions and consult only the primary domain guidance and applicable specializations before implementation or correction. Keep the Approved Slice and authority unchanged; return to this workflow for its execution and evidence rules. Administrative work and documentation without a product change do not load domain guidance by default.

## Inputs

SPEC_PATH, the explicitly selected operation and canonical slice are required. Use the exact activation returned by current inspection for begin; preserve the returned handle for all later commands.

## Minimum Reads

Current requirements, the approved selected Plan/Task, current evidence and blockers, declared product/tests/inputs, and the complete selected runner contract. Read sibling/completed proof only when the current admission requires it.

## Authority

Use `runtime/execution-current-operation.mjs inspect --spec-path SPEC_PATH` and its exact legal handoff. Admission, dispatch, literal capture, candidate composition, publication, readback and own cleanup belong to that controller. Never write operational records, counters, final results or activation directly. Preserve the returned handle and all uncertain effects. No interpreter, repair, migration or persistence adapter for earlier workflow formats is supported. Managed transport is unsupported. Supported REPLAN publication belongs to the bounded planner/reviewer path; this execution controller does not substitute for it.

Requirements and approved boundaries govern semantic scope. Authorized Areas permit effects; Expected Files are predictions. Existing Inputs are read/proof dependencies and confer no write permission. Authentication, persistence and public contract responsibilities require explicit semantic authority in addition to path containment. A mandatory checklist proves implementation progress only. Formal validation alone owns finding disposition and PASS.

Delegate each admitted check/formal to one fresh independent context. Load the complete selected runner contract before scope reads, retain its explicit path/hash, and use the official returned identity and full first finalResponse. Do not suggest the verdict, replace rejected bytes, use session directories, or turn discovery into verification evidence. The reviewed recipe runs only through the returned helper. Read the mandatory [runner invocation guide](references/runner-invocation.md) from this installed skill before selecting or spawning. The origin-bound receipt is mandatory; capture consumes the saved observation without replay.

Only the executor changes product code/tests for its approved implementation or active findings after admission. The quality manager does not implement corrections or create test coverage. A failed verification command remains evidence and cannot be hidden by a later success. Missing sufficient proof blocks PASS. Permission/sandbox denial requires preserving its full diagnostic and stopping the denied action, even when a later shell exit is zero; never retry through another path, tool, temporary setting or permission mode.



## Check preparation and outcomes

Prepare reusable tests in the authorized implementation or correction before delegation, covering every applicable acceptance criterion and its required variants. Include those tests in the final Changed Areas. Writing tests is implementation work; executing verification remains runner-only. Give the runner concrete existing test paths and coverage intent, not an instruction to create an ad hoc script. Build fixtures from the existing input and persistence contracts; preserve the required assertions and variants, and do not expand those contracts to make a fixture valid. An old passing suite that omits the new behavior is insufficient. Do not depend on heredocs, here-strings or nested shell quoting to generate multiline programs: the shell may need an implicit temporary file outside the permitted destination. If a command reports a sandbox or permission denial, stop and preserve the full diagnostic even when its final exit is zero; do not retry via another path, tool, temp setting or permission mode.

Classify delegation outcomes before persistence: initialization/transport failure, valid runner `BLOCKED`, malformed runner output, and valid `TESTS_FAIL` are distinct. An executed verification command failing because of an objective defect in an executor-prepared test, fixture or assertion is `TESTS_FAIL` when the evidence identifies a correction inside the approved slice and existing rights; it need not demonstrate an implementation defect. Preserve the command, exit, violated contract and diagnostic, and do not claim unverified behavior passed. Only the main context may correct after persistence, only when the controller admits another check and the supplied correction margin remains. Sandbox or permission denial, a genuinely unavailable tool, dependency or input, or an objective lack of sufficient runnable checks remains `BLOCKED`; never convert these causes to fixture defects or bypass the denial. A valid `BLOCKED` is a runner result, not a transport failure. Formal `VALIDATE_SLICE` retains its separate `PASS`/`NEEDS_FIX`/`BLOCKED` judgment and evidence requirements.

Additional automatic rounds occur after `TESTS_FAIL` in an admitted nonterminal round and an authorized correction, or after an EXECUTE `BLOCKED` whose optional `testCoverageOmissions` proof the current-operation producer admits. That proof binds an approved obligation, an existing writable recipe-selected test and its inspected SHA-256; commands remain green and the result remains `BLOCKED`. Follow `capture-native`/`resume`: only `reserve-dispatch` permits correction of the identified test files on the same owner within the remaining admitted local margin. No implementation, recipe or authority changes belong to that correction. Other blockers, absent proof, uncertainty and exhausted calls grant no coverage correction or first-preparation Initial publication; revised-preparation dispositions retain their existing contract. Never publish an intermediate admitted omission instead of correcting it. Before the first capture, explicit semantic triage may identify missing structured coverage proof for a valid EXECUTE BLOCKED. Follow only the controller-admitted testCoverageClarification handoff to the same child; this spends an existing round, preserves tested work and BLOCKED, and grants no correction until the returned proof is authenticated. See runner-invocation for the distinct clarification and correction steps. Never make a sixth automatic invocation, fall back to checks in the main context, invent a result, create a formal Validation Attempt or Effective Validation Base, mark a final result or continue automatically to another operation.

Completion of one delivery does not close the execution; inspect for the next pristine delivery. Managed mode, shared/set replacement remains outside this profile.

For an eligible current S2 with preparationRevision > 1 and an approved positive localRevision, route `VALIDATE_SLICE` and a blocking formal's `APPLY_FINDINGS` through `runtime/execution-current-operation.mjs`. ### Current native validation with completed deliveries

The current S2 controller also admits exact `VALIDATE_SLICE` and `APPLY_FINDINGS` handoffs with completed deliveries when the selected active delivery has an approved positive localRevision and verified native formal/findings sources with A0/A1 admission. Validation requires a closed PASS base or concluded findings checks. APPLY requires a blocking NEEDS_FIX and an unchanged pre-edit baseline. Completed proofs must remain current. Preserve the frozen snapshot and every literal evidence decision: the new active proof is established by its current snapshot, authority, owner, request and native admission chain, rather than a rewritten review or a historical-read exception. The offline capture factory does not admit this operation.

Select and hash the complete candidate-distribution runner contract before dispatch and recheck that same file after controller restarts. Load it in the independent agent before any scope reads or checks. Use the explicit native judgment variant, official identity and complete first final response. PASS followed by private authorized drift may admit another validation. A valid NEEDS_FIX publishes its active findings and admits the exact APPLY_FINDINGS handoff before product corrections. The completed profile consumes the existing findings cycle and requests a new independent formal validation after a concluded auxiliary result; only that formal may resolve findings. Ordinary TESTS_FAIL retries, one proved private product-drift episode and concluded auxiliary-result rejection use the existing invocation/episode budget, without reset. A rejected auxiliary result must be published and cleaned up before one explicit successor can use the remaining calls; uncertain effects, foreign identity/HEAD/observation and an exhausted episode block. A valid BLOCKED remains blocking unless the principal proves the explicit incomplete-review disposition; an external blocker is never recoverable by that path. Recipe or input authority changes require local review and are not implementation corrections under the old approval. An uncertain response preserves its literal capture and A1 owner, with resume returning stop-and-diagnose. A concluded, bound schema/semantic refusal follows the bounded normalization below. Identified partial-candidate recovery after a valid capture still reuses the same bytes without a runner or recipe. Readback must work from self-contained proof after transient controls become unavailable. An integral judgment requires the explicit terminal request described in the mandatory runner guide; Q33, physical REPLAN and CLOSE remain outside this profile.


### Current pristine execution and private review with completed

Use the existing current-operation controller for an exact pristine EXECUTE_SLICE handoff. Pristine requires unvalidated, no evidence, zero counters, no diagnostic or normalization, unchanged baseline, and no operation or cleanup pending. A first operational proof has no preceding evidenceDecision; it must verify its native sources, current authority, full approved snapshot and A0/A1 admission. Do not treat the absence of a decision as retained proof or manufacture a review decision for it. Native implementation checks and their first formal validation use the existing evidence producers and response variants. Retry TESTS_FAIL only within the five admitted implementation invocations; persistence recovery reuses the same captured bytes. A single proved private product-drift episode may consume the remaining invocation budget. Valid BLOCKED and uncertain conclusions grant no handoff.

Private recipe/input changes use prepareCurrentLocalReview/finalizeCurrentLocalReview and publishCurrentLocalReview. Present the exact candidate to an independent reviewer before publication. Review can precede pristine execution and can follow an earlier private review; preserve UUIDs, global review, completed proofs, counters and literal source records. Revalidate prior proof under the revised recipe. Reject stale approval and shared/set impact with REPLAN_REQUIRED. New native origins preserve other active identities in the existing otherDeliveryWork observation; old sources lacking a necessary historical owner binding stay blocked after the corresponding carrier changes. Do not reconstruct that identity from the current selector map alone.

A concluded, origin-bound EXECUTE or APPLY rejection keeps its complete predecessor chain. The controller determines the remaining eligible calls and successor owner. Request `round` is the global invocation ordinal; origin, receipt and baseline ordinals remain local to their owner, and semantic `automaticCheckRound` follows the request. See the mandatory runner invocation guide. Persistence recovery never admits another model call or owner.

Concluded formal recovery follows the bounded path in the mandatory runner invocation guide for recognized v1/v2/v3/v4 native requests, including Initial and completed. A schema-invalid model response creates no formal ID or verdict and is never partially accepted. An eligible successor remains independent. A schema-valid incomplete BLOCKED review requires explicit principal triage of concrete missing canonical work, available inputs and no external impediment. Valid NEEDS_FIX follows product/findings correction; untriaged BLOCKED remains blocking. Preserve literal responses, diagnostics, triage and new requests. Honor the supplied remaining margin across correction categories and identities; stop recurrence without demonstrated correction. Replay starts no new turn or recipe. Save complete finalResponse as literal UTF-8 and create its tool envelope with JSON.stringify, never hand-escaped nested text.

Before an authorized EXECUTE/APPLY effect, the controller captures existing removal-proof-v1 bytes and mode for private output candidates in its own namespace. Incorporation selects exactly the files actually removed; missing, modified or foreign proofs block. Existing Inputs and other deliveries remain protected. A following formal verifies REMOVED against the retained proof. Preserve literal sources while needed; compact superseded closed cycles only at the next valid boundary, keeping high-water counters. Paths with spaces remain typed recipe arguments and quoted CLI arguments.

Converted v3/v4 and recognized imported predecessors use the existing local-review approval and publisher. Repeated private reviews require independent approval of each exact candidate and fresh formal proof, preserving UUIDs, other deliveries and globalReview. A current local revalidate decision governs only its active delivery; it does not rewrite the frozen set review or retain a changed completed proof. Equivalent native sibling sources remain valid only after full original proof, exact local contract and observed product/ownership checks. This integration does not claim a physical producer of completed, managed transport, Q33, CLOSE or closure of items 5/6. Integral judgment uses the explicit terminal request and current-proof checks in the mandatory runner guide.

## Allowed Effects

Use only the current producer-owned operation and its approved scope. The executor may edit authorized product/tests after admission; the quality manager owns only the formal result through the current producer. Preserve all other authority and evidence.

## Blocks

Missing or stale authority/proof, uncertain identity or effects, an active conflicting owner, unsafe paths, exhausted budgets, missing independent contract/context, denied actions or an unavailable capability block the operation. Preserve their exact diagnostics.

## Output

Read back through `inspect` after publication. Report operation/slice, observed result, exact evidence location, publication and cleanup separately, current handoff, remaining blockers and changed areas. A completed delivery does not automatically close the execution, SPEC, U3, or acceptance items. Stop without selecting another slice.

CLI arguments are closed per command: begin always requires explicit --operation; reserve-dispatch accepts --terminal as a flag with no value only for an intended integral formal judgment. Unknown, duplicate, missing-value and extra arguments are errors before admission; do not rely on ignored tokens or a default operation. A quiet inspect refusing an active owner is not evidence of lost ownership: use the saved handle and resume to distinguish captured TESTS_FAIL (authorized correction then next ordinary round, up to five) from running, uncertain, rejected or BLOCKED results. A presentation change never permits rewriting captured bytes or bypassing a binding.

Match each assertion to the approved observable contract: prose reflow, equivalent example formatting and object property enumeration order are not requirements unless the authority says so. Preserve required group order, exact keys/paths/identities and executable examples. Mechanical lexical checks are bounded supplements; they cannot alone certify the truth of documentation. Formal validation must read the documented behavior and examples, including missing or contradictory claims, without forcing a private phrase or marker into the product contract.
