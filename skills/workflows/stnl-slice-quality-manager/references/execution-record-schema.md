# Current operational records

The current closed image and evidence schemas are implemented in execution-current.mjs, execution-preparation-current.mjs and current-evidence-state.mjs. Use their existing producers and readback. Do not author activation, continuity, records, counters, capture hashes or final manifests manually.

A native operation seals its current authority, owner, dispatch and observed baseline before effects. Capture saves the complete first concluded response unchanged and binds the official returned agent identity, request and reviewed recipe observation. Publication verifies that proof before deriving the selected Task, index, evidence and new activation atomically. Evidence for completed deliveries and prior private reviews remains literal and independently verifiable.

Persistence recovery uses the original handle and captured bytes without another agent or recipe. Concluded malformed-result successors require explicit current normalization and inherit the same episode and remaining invocation budget. Uncertain effects, foreign identity, stale authority/proof and exhausted budgets block. Cleanup is limited to that operation's positively owned roles after confirmed readback.

Earlier Markdown record aliases, historical repair, imported receipts, old preparation conversion and managed persistence adapters are not supported. Preserve unsupported or uncertain material for diagnosis; do not reinterpret it or reset authority.
